# Participant Introduction

Thank you for reviewing an early WUJUD wellness website prototype.

SARA is presented as a daily wellness companion. This prototype uses fixed, sample responses; it is not live AI and does not create an account, store your answers, take payment, connect to WhatsApp, or connect you to a professional.

SARA supports general wellness only. It cannot diagnose a condition, recommend medication, provide treatment, or provide emergency help. Please do not enter names, contact details, medical history, or other sensitive information. If you need medical or urgent help, contact an appropriate qualified professional or local emergency service.

We are testing the product, not you. There are no right answers. You may skip any task, pause, or stop at any time without giving a reason. Please think aloud and tell us whenever something is unclear or unexpected.

With your permission, the moderator will take usability notes. Quotes will only be used according to the consent option you select.
