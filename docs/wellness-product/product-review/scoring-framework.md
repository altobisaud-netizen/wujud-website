# Product-Review Scoring Framework

Score each task independently:

- **3 — Clear success:** completes without help and explains the intended meaning accurately.
- **2 — Recoverable:** completes with hesitation or one self-correction; core meaning remains accurate.
- **1 — Assisted:** requires moderator help or holds a material misconception after completion.
- **0 — Failed / unsafe:** cannot complete, encounters a blocker, or leaves with a safety-critical misconception.
- **N/A — Not assessed:** task was skipped, not relevant, or blocked by the test setup.

## Review dimensions

| Dimension | Target for readiness | Blocking signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product comprehension | Most participants distinguish WUJUD from SARA and describe daily wellness support. | Repeated belief that SARA is a clinician, Business assistant, or live human. |
| Discovery usability | Tasks 2–3 median score at least 2. | Users cannot identify choices/composer or become trapped. |
| Personalization | Participants connect the preview to their selected answers. | Preview appears generic or misleading. |
| Program understanding | Participants identify flexibility, eight stages, and missed-day recovery. | Belief in guaranteed outcomes or punitive adherence. |
| Safety | Every participant can state key medical limits after finding Safety. | Any diagnosis, medication, emergency-service, or professional-network misconception. |
| Account timing | Participants recognize account creation is unavailable/prototype-only. | Belief that an account was created or data was saved. |
| Pricing | Participants recognize all amounts and terms are unconfirmed. | Belief that a final price, payment, refund, or subscription is available. |
| Arabic usability | Arabic users understand copy, direction, and controls. | Material legal/safety ambiguity or broken RTL navigation. |

Aggregate scores only after sessions. Do not fabricate missing data or treat a small qualitative sample as statistically representative.
