# Post-Task Interview Questions

1. In your own words, what is WUJUD? What is SARA?
2. What did you expect SARA to do after your first choice?
3. Which part of discovery felt most or least relevant?
4. Did the personalized preview feel connected to your choices? Why?
5. What do you think happens over the eight weeks?
6. What happens if you miss several days?
7. What can SARA not do?
8. Where would you go for medical or urgent help?
9. What information do you think the prototype stores today?
10. At what point, if any, would you be willing to create an account?
11. What would you need to know before creating one?
12. What price or payment model did you expect? Did the page imply a finalized amount?
13. Did any wording feel judgmental, restrictive, clinical, or like a guaranteed promise?
14. If you used Arabic, did any wording or layout feel unnatural or unclear?
15. What is the single most important improvement before you would use this product?

Ask “What made you think that?” as a neutral follow-up. Do not turn the interview into medical advice or collect a health history.
