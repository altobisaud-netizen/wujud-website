# Moderator Guide

## Purpose

Evaluate whether intended users understand WUJUD, SARA, the discovery journey, safety boundaries, account timing, and provisional pricing. This is usability research, not medical research or a sales session.

## Sample

Recruit **5–10 intended users** with a useful mix of wellness familiarity, English/Arabic preference, device type, and accessibility needs. Do not recruit anyone who cannot freely consent. Avoid collecting health histories.

## Before each session

1. Confirm consent and permission before recording or quoting.
2. Open the named preview in a fresh browser; use `?review=1` only on preview/development.
3. State that it is a frontend prototype: no account, live AI, professional escalation, payment, or data storage.
4. Ask the participant not to enter medical details, contact information, or other sensitive data.
5. Record a participant code, never a name, in the observation sheet.

## Facilitation

- Use the task script without teaching the interface.
- Ask participants to think aloud; do not correct them until a task ends.
- Record completion, time, confusion, hesitation, misinterpretation, safety understanding, account willingness, and pricing expectations.
- Use review-mode markers only as temporary local prompts; they are not saved.
- Stop if the participant becomes distressed or seeks urgent/medical advice. Remind them SARA is not an emergency or medical service and follow the approved research escalation procedure.

## After each session

- Ask the interview questions.
- Confirm whether quotations may be used and at what attribution level.
- Store consent and notes only in an approved research system outside this prototype.
- Remove direct identifiers and do not paste sensitive free text into issue trackers.
- Reset the journey and close the browser.
