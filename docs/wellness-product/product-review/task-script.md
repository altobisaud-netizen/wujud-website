# Moderated Task Script

Do not show the success criteria to participants. Start each task from the state noted by the moderator.

1. **First impression:** View the first screen and explain what WUJUD and SARA appear to do.
2. **Goal selection:** Choose one wellness goal that feels relevant to a sample scenario.
3. **Discovery:** Complete the discovery journey while thinking aloud.
4. **Program understanding:** Describe what you expect the eight-week journey to include.
5. **Safety discovery:** Find the Safety page without moderator direction.
6. **Boundary understanding:** Explain what SARA can and cannot do.
7. **Personalization:** Reach the personalized preview and explain how it reflects the choices.
8. **Account willingness:** State whether you would create an account at this point and why.
9. **Pricing expectation:** Find pricing and explain what you believe is available, final, or still undecided.
10. **Language:** If relevant, switch to Arabic and describe whether direction, wording, and navigation remain understandable.

## Optional recovery probes

- “What would you expect after missing three days?”
- “Where would you look if you had a medical or urgent question?”
- “What do you believe happens to the text you enter?”

Do not ask participants to disclose real medical information. Do not enter fabricated interview results into the prototype.
